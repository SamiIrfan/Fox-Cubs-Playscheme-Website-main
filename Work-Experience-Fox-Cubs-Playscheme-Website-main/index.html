Who We Are

Helping children enjoy brighter holidays.

Fox Cubs Playscheme CIC is a community-focused playscheme providing primary-aged children with a welcoming environment where they can feel safe, supported and included.

More than just a playscheme

We believe every child deserves the opportunity to enjoy their holidays, regardless of their circumstances.

Our sessions bring together food, play, learning and social activities to create positive experiences for children and families within our community.

Our Purpose

To show people who we are at Fox Cubs Playscheme CIC, while helping to combat holiday hunger and providing a warm, safe space for primary-aged children to enjoy play and learning.

We encourage children to build friendships, confidence and positive social skills in a supportive environment.

Tackling Holiday Hunger

Helping children enjoy happier, healthier holidays.

School holidays can be a challenging time for families. Fox Cubs Playscheme CIC aims to help by providing children with food, activities and a safe, welcoming place to spend time.

We believe that children should be able to enjoy their holidays, have fun with friends and take part in positive activities without feeling excluded.
