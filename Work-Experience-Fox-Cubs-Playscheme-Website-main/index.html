WHY OUR WORK MATTERS

Help tackle holiday hunger.

School holidays can be challenging for families who rely on the support and routines that school provides. Holiday hunger is the difficulty some children face accessing enough nutritious food during these periods.

Fox Cubs Playscheme CIC aims to help address this challenge by supporting access to food alongside opportunities for play, learning and positive social experiences.

✓

Supporting children's wellbeing

Recognising the importance of food, care and positive experiences during the holidays.

✓

Making holidays more enjoyable

Creating opportunities for children to play, explore and connect with others.

✓

Encouraging inclusion

Helping children feel welcome, valued and part of a community.
